How It Works

  • 1. Draw Note: Select the pencil, click on an empty grid slot to place a 200ms note. Click and drag the right edge to extend or shorten its duration.
  • 2. Move Note: Click on the body of a note and drag it to shift either its pitch (up/down) or start-time (left/right).
  • 3. No Overlaps: The editor automatically manages overlaps. Drawing or dropping a note onto another cuts or clips it, keeping the music monophonic!
  • 4. Buzzer Simulator: Click Play Sim to hear your melody utilizing high-fidelity Web Audio oscillators configured to sound exactly like Arduino piezos.
